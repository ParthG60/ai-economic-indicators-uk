# Dashboard design system — directions and tokens

Four candidate identities for the UK AI Economic Indicators dashboard, each applied to the
same real dashboard slice in `dashboard/design-variants/` (generated by `src/design_variants.py`).
Grounded in how comparable bodies present work: Stanford Digital Economy Lab (light, cardinal),
IFS / Resolution Foundation / UK AISI (navy + teal), GOV.UK / ONS (flat official).

The token sets below ARE the style guide: pick one, and `build_dashboard.py` adopts these values
(it already centralises FONT + palette constants, so swapping is a small edit).

## Directions

| Key | Identity | Mode | Display / body | Accent | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `whitepaper` | warm academic (status quo, refined) | light, warm paper | Fraunces / IBM Plex Sans | ink-blue `#1c4e80` | research-note feel; closest to Stanford DEL |
| `institute` | UK think-tank | light, white | Bricolage Grotesque / Hanken Grotesk | teal `#0e7c86` on navy `#0b1f33` | **recommended** — serious, modern, print-safe |
| `lab` | AISI dark | dark | Schibsted Grotesk / Hanken Grotesk | cyan-teal `#35d0c6` | distinctive hero; risky for dense data/print |
| `civic` | GOV.UK / ONS | light, flat | Public Sans | gov-blue `#1d70b8`, focus `#ffdd00` | maximal officialness; utilitarian, implies gov status |

## Judgement (for a UK economics institute)

- **Institute** is the strongest fit: it is the literal visual language of IFS, the Resolution
  Foundation and AISI (navy structure, single confident teal accent), light enough for dense
  tables, screenshots and print, modern without being trendy. Recommended house style.
- **Whitepaper** is the safe scholarly choice and reads as a *paper* more than an *institution*;
  keep as the fallback / long-form-report variant.
- **Lab** is the most memorable but dark mode hurts dense data, printing and report screenshots;
  better reserved for a hero or landing page than the workhorse dashboard.
- **Civic** carries the most authority but is visually flat and, by borrowing GOV.UK styling, risks
  implying official government status this project does not have. Avoid as the primary skin.

**APPLIED (2026-06-25):** the **Institute** identity is now live in `build_dashboard.py` — navy ink
`#0b1f33`, teal accent `#0e7c86`, Bricolage Grotesque headings / Hanken Grotesk body, soft-shadowed
cards at radius 11. Data series use a **colour-blind-safe Okabe-Ito** palette. Two-series comparisons
(most- vs least-exposed, automation vs augmentation, on-site Q5 vs Q1) use the canonical **dark blue
`#0072B2` vs orange `#E69F00`** pair: these differ in *lightness* as well as hue, so they stay
separable under deuteranopia and protanopia (verified by Machado-2009 simulation) where blue-vs-vermillion,
sitting at similar lightness, is weaker. Teal `#009E73` and vermillion `#D55E00` fill secondary roles; the
heatmap uses a lightness-ordered teal ramp. Hover labels are forced to white background + navy text so
they're readable over any series colour.
